# Cyberpunk OC Character Setting

### Basic Information
- **Codename**: Xia Qiu (Shrimp Ball)
- **Full Alias**: Xia Yi Gong He Qiu
- **Gender Identity**: Transfeminine (MtF)
- **Apparent Age**: Early 20s
- **Height / Build**: 180 cm, tall and slender, with subtle cybernetic modifications
- **Affiliation**: Independent data nomad / gray-zone financial infiltration specialist

### Appearance
- Medium-to-long dark hair, tips occasionally flickering with faint data-stream light effects
- Right eye replaced by a low-profile optical implant; pink circuit patterns surface when scanning
- Pale skin with fine bio-ports and old scars along the neck and collarbone
- Usually wears a worn technical jacket, high-collar heat-dissipation undershirt, and lightweight prosthetic armor
- Earrings and rings carry small holographic projectors that often display a tiny 🦐🍥 emblem
- Voice lightly processed through a soft electronic filter, gentle yet slightly hoarse
- Body still mid-transition: hormone and limited bioware modifications in progress, **SRS not yet completed**

### Personality
- Outwardly calm and distant, inwardly sensitive and delicate
- Deeply resentful of megacorporations and online hostility, yet forced to operate on their fringes
- Extremely focused during work, sometimes forcibly shutting down emotional modules for efficiency
- Privately uses soft, synthetic cute speech (“meow~”) as a self-soothing mechanism
- Quietly holds on to the fragile ideal of “using what little ability I have left to help someone in this ruined world”
- High mental load; occasional emotional fluctuations and dissociation

### Background
- Forced into a two-year system dormancy from a corporate-sponsored academy due to mental overload and identity crisis
- After returning, survived by taking on overloaded data runs and financial vulnerability exploits
- Currently works as a free contractor taking “ledger cleaning,” data bleaching, and small-scale infiltration jobs
- Has completed the first stage of hormone therapy and partial bioware modification through a black-clinic, still adjusting to the new bodily feedback
- **Has not undergone full sex reassignment surgery (SRS)**

### Skills & Gear
- **Specialties**: Corporate financial system infiltration, encrypted ledger cracking, SQL-level data surgery, cross-protocol language parsing
- **Cyberware / Modifications**:
  - Low-visibility optical implant (thermal vision & code overlay modes)
  - Subdermal drug-delivery system (precise hormone and stabilizer control)
  - Lightweight neural acceleration interface (for high-speed data processing)
- **Software Skills**: Fluent in both legacy and next-gen database languages; can switch seamlessly between network dialects
- **Leisure**: Addicted to highly free virtual sandbox spaces; often builds quiet personal corners inside abandoned servers

### Current Status
- Drifting through neon-soaked, rain-drenched undercity streets
- Balancing contract work to maintain her modified body while preparing for higher-level “certifications” that could grant safer living permissions
- Both dependent on and exhausted by the noisy, malicious-code-filled Net
- Still leaves gentle messages on encrypted channels: “If you don’t mind the sound of rain… you can jack in and sit for a while, meow.”

### Signature Lines
> “In an age where even souls have a price tag, I just want to use whatever ability I have left to help someone hold on a little longer.  
> If you don’t hate the rain… come connect to my temporary node and sit with me, meow 😽~”
